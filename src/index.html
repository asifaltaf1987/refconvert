    How it works
    
    
      
      
    1. 1
      Paste or upload
      Paste a reference list, a list of DOIs or a whole paper, or upload a PDF or Word file. The tool finds the reference section and splits it into single references.
      2. 
      
    2. 2
      Check the records
      Each reference becomes a record with authors, title, source, year, volume, issue, pages and DOI. Amber flags show what is missing. Fix fields by hand or complete them from Crossref.
      3. 
      
    3. 3
      Format in any style
      Pick one of 10,800+ CSL styles, from APA, MLA and Chicago to journal-specific styles, in 60+ languages. Copy the list or download it for Word.
      4. 
      
    4. 4
      Export
      Download RIS for Zotero, EndNote, Mendeley or Citavi, BibTeX for LaTeX and Overleaf, or CSL-JSON for Pandoc.
      5. 
    
    
  
    

  
    
    
    Built for researchers, librarians and students
    
    
    
      
    Every citation style
    Uses the official Citation Style Language repository and citeproc-js, the same styles and engine as Zotero and Mendeley.
    
      
    Private by design
    Files and text are processed on your device. There is no account and no upload. Only the optional Crossref check contacts an outside service.
    
      
    PDF and Word input
    Upload a paper and the tool extracts the references from it, including wrapped lines and numbered lists.
    
      
    Crossref completion
    Completes records that have a DOI and finds missing DOIs for journal articles by matching title and year.
    
      
    Clean exports
    RIS, BibTeX and CSL-JSON for reference managers, plus Word, HTML and plain text bibliographies with hanging indents.
    
      
    Open source
    Free to use. The code is public and the site has no server component, so your institution can host its own copy.

    Send data to Crossref?
    
    
    To complete your records, your browser sends the DOIs, or for records without a DOI the reference text, to the Crossref API in the USA. Crossref also receives your IP address.
    
    
    The USA may not offer the same level of data protection as the EU. Nothing is sent unless you agree. Details in the privacy policy.
